# Half-Life 2 VR Standalone 0.989

- Rebuilt both complete VR renderer runtimes from the latest committed `main` source.
- Improved room-scale locomotion, seated body tracking and physical interactions.
- Added synchronized VR teleport handling for the client and server.
- Improved physical crowbar, reload-contact and support-hand interaction behavior.
- Corrected VR projection and zoom state in Citadel pods and vehicles.
- Half-Life 2, Lost Coast, Episode One, Episode Two and Portal 1 remain supported in one APK.
- Portal 2 remains hidden and is not offered by the launcher or cache builder.
- Fixed the complete distribution packaging so `vr_game_resources/MANIFEST.sha256` is always included and checked before cache generation.
- Updated the launcher and documentation support link to `https://dalink.to/zer0k0`.
