# Half-Life 2 VR Standalone 0.985

- One APK and one `/sdcard/srceng` data root for Half-Life 2, Lost Coast, Episode One, Episode Two and Portal 1.
- Portal 1 added to the launcher with campaign-aware saves and current VR interaction fixes.
- Hybrid and Turbo launch modes are available; Turbo also supports Portal 1.
- Windows and Linux cache builders can import a legal Portal installation into the shared cache.
- Portal 2 is intentionally not displayed and is not included in the build.
