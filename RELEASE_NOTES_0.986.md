# Half-Life 2 VR Standalone 0.986

- One APK and one `/sdcard/srceng` data root for Half-Life 2, Lost Coast, Episode One, Episode Two and Portal 1.
- Portal 1 includes campaign-aware saves and current VR interaction fixes.
- Hybrid and Turbo launch modes remain available for Half-Life 2 and its episodes. Portal 1 is locked to Hybrid because its Turbo runtime is not release-safe.
- Windows and Linux cache builders include and verify the complete Portal compatibility shader set, preventing white menu and world materials in generated caches.
- Portal 2 is intentionally not displayed and is not included in the build.
