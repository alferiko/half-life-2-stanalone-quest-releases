# Half-Life 2 VR Standalone 0.988

- One APK and one `/sdcard/srceng` data root for Half-Life 2, Lost Coast, Episode One, Episode Two and Portal 1.
- Added immersive room-scale locomotion and continuous crouching.
- Added VR laser sights and universal X+Y calibration.
- Support-hand pickup and physical reload state now remain independent across weapon switches.
- Includes accepted material color-space handling, VR damage/effect fixes and the static-prop decal crash fix.
- Hybrid and Turbo launch modes remain available for Half-Life 2 and its episodes. Portal 1 is locked to Hybrid because its Turbo runtime is not release-safe.
- Windows and Linux cache builders include and verify the complete Portal compatibility shader set.
- Portal 2 remains hidden and is not offered by the launcher or cache builder.
